Frequently asked questions

I don't feel it doing anything. Is it working?

Everything you could feel — the sting, the burn — was force on the wrong thing. Light is fuel, and fuel is silent. Judge it in the mornings, over a couple of weeks. That's why you get 90 nights, not 14 days.

Will it fit smaller nostrils?

Yes. The soft silicone tips fit most nostrils comfortably, and they rest just inside the opening — never deep. If yours are smaller, the device can also be used without the sleeves for a closer fit.

Can I use it with a deviated septum?

Yes, for comfort — but being straight with you: it won't fix the structural issue. It supports the swollen lining, a separate part of what blocks your nights.

Can I breathe with it in my nose?

Completely normally. The tips don't seal the airflow — most people read through the three minutes.

Will the red light bother my eyes or keep me awake?

The light shines inside the nasal passages, not at your eyes. Most people use it right before bed with no effect on falling asleep.

Is it rechargeable? Does it get hot?

USB rechargeable — no batteries to buy, and a charge lasts many sessions. It emits no heat and no UV; most people feel nothing at all during use.

Is it safe for kids, or during pregnancy?

It's gentle — no UV, no heat. Still: for kids, pregnancy, or any medical condition, check with your doctor first.

Can I keep using my sprays or medications with it?

Yes — there's nothing chemical in the light. But never change a medication routine on your own: do it with your doctor or pharmacist. The included e-book covers this honestly.
