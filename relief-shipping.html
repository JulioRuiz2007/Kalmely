Shipping policy.

Last updated · June 23, 2026

Shipping is free on every Kalmely Relief order within the United States. Most US orders arrive in about 7–15 business days, and a tracking link is emailed to you as soon as your order ships.

Where we ship

We ship to all 50 US states. Once your order is processed it's prepared and dispatched, and you'll receive an email with a tracking link so you can follow it on its way to you.

How long does it take?

ProcessingOrders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after you place them.
TransitOnce shipped, US delivery usually takes 7–15 business days.
Total estimateMost US orders arrive within about 7–15 business days from when they ship — occasionally up to ~20 business days during busy periods or customs delays.

These are estimates, not guarantees. Delivery windows can run longer during holidays, sale periods, or because of carrier and customs delays that are outside our control. We'll always email your tracking so you can see exactly where your order is.

Shipping cost

Shipping is free on every order — the single jar, the 2-jar pack, and the 2-jar bundle with free gifts. There's no minimum and no paid express tier. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link that updates as your parcel moves through the carrier network. If your order has shipped but you haven't received a tracking link, please write to support@kalmely.com and we'll resend it.

Subscription orders

The 2-jar bundle (with free gifts) is a Subscribe & Save plan that automatically renews every 2 months, and each renewal ships with free shipping and a fresh tracking link. We email you before each renewal, and you can cancel anytime through the self-serve portal link in any order email, by emailing support@kalmely.com, or by calling 1-800-555-0188. Full details are in our Returns & Refund Policy.

If your order is delayed

If your tracking hasn't updated in a while, or your order is taking longer than estimated, please allow a little extra time, as customs and carrier handoffs can add a few days. If it's been notably longer than expected, email support@kalmely.com with your order number and we'll look into it with the carrier.

If your order doesn't arrive

If tracking shows the parcel as delivered but you don't have it, please check with anyone else at your address and look for a "safe place" delivery photo. If it's still missing, email support@kalmely.com with your order number and we'll open an investigation with the carrier. If the parcel can't be located, we'll arrange a replacement or a refund.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged or you received the wrong item, photograph the box and contents and email us at support@kalmely.com within a few days of delivery. We'll make it right with a free replacement and won't ask you to return the damaged item.

Changes to your delivery address

If you spot an error in your shipping address, contact us as soon as possible after ordering and we'll do our best to correct it before the order ships. Once it has shipped we may not be able to reroute it, but we'll work with the carrier to help where we can.

Questions about shipping? Email support@kalmely.com or call 1-800-555-0188 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET). We answer every message, usually within one business day.
