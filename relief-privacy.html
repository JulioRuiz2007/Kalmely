Privacy policy.

Last updated · June 23, 2026

This policy explains what information Kalmely ("we", "us") collects when you visit our store or place an order for Kalmely Relief, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

Kalmely, 1209 N Orange St, Wilmington, DE 19801, USA. Questions about your data: support@kalmely.com or 1-800-555-0188.

What we collect

  • Order & contact details — name, shipping address, email, phone number.
  • Payment information — handled by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not see or store your full card number; we receive only a token and the last four digits.
  • Usage & device data — IP address, browser, pages viewed, and similar analytics, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

How we use it

  • To process, ship, and support your order, and to send order and tracking emails.
  • To provide customer support by email and phone.
  • To send marketing emails only if you opt in; every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • To prevent fraud, comply with the law, and improve the store.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers we need to run the store, under contract and only for that purpose:

  • Payment — Stripe (card processing & refunds).
  • Fulfillment & shipping — our warehouse/fulfillment partner and the carriers, to deliver your order.
  • Email — our email provider, to send order and (if you opted in) marketing emails.
  • Analytics & advertising — providers such as Google Analytics and Meta (Facebook) that help us measure and improve our ads, where enabled.

We do not sell your personal information.

Cookies & tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep the store working, remember your cart, and measure how our ads perform. You can control cookies in your browser settings; blocking some may affect how the store works.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, and to stop marketing emails, at any time — email support@kalmely.com. If you're a California resident, you have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of "sales/sharing" of personal information (we don't sell your data). We won't discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed to fulfill your orders, support you, and meet legal and accounting obligations, then delete or anonymize it. We use industry-standard safeguards (including encrypted connections and PCI-compliant payment processing) to protect your data.

Children

Our store is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest version. Material changes will be posted here.
