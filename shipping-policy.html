Shipping policy.

Last updated · June 19, 2026

Shipping is free on every Kalmely Tea Tree Oil order. We ship to addresses across the United States, with typical delivery in 10–20 business days. A tracking link is emailed to you as soon as your order ships.

Where we ship

Kalmely ships internationally, including to all 50 US states. Once your order is processed, it's prepared and dispatched, and you'll receive an email with a tracking link so you can follow it on its way to you.

How long does it take?

  • Processing: orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after you place them.
  • Transit: once shipped, delivery to the United States usually takes 10–20 business days.
  • Total: most US orders arrive within about 10–20 business days from when they ship.

Delivery times are estimates and can occasionally run longer during busy periods or because of carrier or customs delays outside our control.

Shipping cost

Shipping is free on every order — the 30 ml bottle, the 60 ml bottle, and the 100 ml bundle with 4 free gifts. There's no minimum and no paid express tier. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay.

Tracking

When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. The link updates as your parcel moves through the carrier network, so you can see where it is at any time. If your order has shipped but you haven't received a tracking link, please write to hello@kalmely.com and we'll resend it.

Subscription orders

The 100 ml bundle is a Subscribe & Save plan that automatically renews every 2 months, and each renewal ships to you with free shipping and a fresh tracking link. You can cancel anytime through the self-serve customer portal (the link is in your order confirmation email) or by emailing hello@kalmely.com.

What if my order is delayed?

If your tracking hasn't updated in a while, or your order is taking longer than the estimated 10–20 business days, please give it a little extra time, as customs and carrier handoffs can add a few days. If it's been notably longer than expected, email hello@kalmely.com with your order number and we'll look into it with the carrier.

What if my order doesn't arrive?

If your tracking shows the parcel as delivered but you don't have it, please check with anyone else at your address and look for a "safe place" delivery photo (carriers often photograph where they left it). If the parcel is still missing, email hello@kalmely.com with your order number and we'll open an investigation with the carrier. If the parcel can't be located, we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Damaged or incorrect items

If your order arrives damaged, or you received the wrong item, photograph the box and contents and email us at hello@kalmely.com within a few days of delivery. We'll make it right with a free replacement and won't ask you to return the damaged item.

Changes to your delivery address

If you spot an error in your shipping address, contact us as soon as possible after placing your order. We'll do our best to correct it before the order ships. Once it has shipped, we may not be able to reroute it, but we'll work with the carrier to help where we can.

Anything unclear? Email hello@kalmely.com. We answer every message, usually within one business day.
