How long does the battery last?
Around 16 hours of playback on a full charge, easily a long-haul flight, or several shorter trips between charges. It recharges over USB-C in about 2–3 hours.
Does it work with any phone?
Yes. The speakers connect over standard Bluetooth, so they pair with any iPhone, Android phone or tablet. Play music, rain, white noise or a podcast, whatever helps you drift off.
Is it comfortable for side sleepers?
That's exactly who it's built for. The speakers are ultra-thin and flat and the fabric is soft, so there's nothing hard pressing into your ear when you turn onto your side.
Does it really block the light fully?
Yes, the raised 3D cups seal out 100% of the light without touching your eyes, including cabin lights and a neighbour's screen. You can open your eyes inside it and still see only black.
Can I wash it?
Yes. Remove the slim electronics and the mask is fully washable, so it stays fresh trip after trip.
