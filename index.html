The mechanism

Density starts at the scalp — not the strand.

For years women over 50 were told the same thing: blame your age, your hormones, your genes. But a growing body of research has turned its attention to one place almost no one was looking — the environment of the scalp itself. And for many women, what's happening up there may not be only hormones.

Meet The Grease Cap.

Day after day, a hardened layer of greasy buildup forms over the roots and seals them shut — so the root underneath can't breathe. Picture a lid pressed down over the follicle. Nothing reaches the root. Nothing escapes. The root sits there, smothered and choked, under a cap it can't lift on its own.

Why everything else failed

This is why the biotin didn't move the needle. Why the serums, the dropper, the $88-a-month supplements did nothing. They didn't fail because of you. They landed on a sealed scalp and just sat there — never reaching the root they were meant to feed.

Why daily shampoo can't fix it

And daily liquid shampoo? It only strips the surface. The scalp panics, makes more oil, and the cycle starts again. It never dissolves the hardened cap. It was never built to.

The fix the shape begs for

A cap doesn't need to be scrubbed. It needs to be lifted. Our bar is made with fermented botanical oils designed to help break down that hardened layer instead of scrubbing at fragile roots — so the Grease Cap can lift and the scalp can feel able to breathe again. It's the missing first step. The one that goes before everything else you've already tried.
