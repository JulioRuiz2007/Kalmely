Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

How does the Scalp Revival Bar actually work?

Most shampoos clean your hair but leave the scalp coated in sebum, styling product and hard-water residue — the buildup that leaves follicles congested and hair looking flat. Our solid bar is built for the scalp first. Fermented botanicals and gentle clarifying actives help dissolve that buildup, soothe itch and flaking, and create a fresh, balanced foundation so hair feels thicker, fuller and sheds less over time.

Is it safe, and who is it for?

Yes. The bar is vegan, sulfate-free and made without harsh chemicals or drying detergents — gentle enough for sensitive, itchy or flaky scalps. It's designed for anyone dealing with buildup, oiliness, itch or limp, thinning-feeling hair. If you have a known scalp condition or allergies, check the ingredient list and chat with your doctor first.

How long does one bar last?

With normal use — a few washes a week — a single bar typically lasts around 2 to 3 months, depending on hair length and how often you wash. Because you glide it directly on the scalp instead of squeezing out handfuls of liquid shampoo, a little goes a long way. That's why most people grab the 2- or 3-bar bundle.

Can I still use it with my shampoo, conditioner and serums?

Absolutely. The bar works beautifully on its own, but it also plays nicely with the rest of your routine. Use it as your main scalp cleanse, then follow with conditioner on your lengths if you like. And because it clears away buildup, any leave-in serums you love can actually reach the scalp instead of sitting on top of a layer of residue.

How is this different from my regular shampoo?

Regular shampoo is made to clean hair strands, often with foaming sulfates that strip moisture and leave the scalp out of the equation. The Scalp Revival Bar flips the focus to the scalp — where healthy, full-feeling hair really starts. It's concentrated, low-waste and free of harsh detergents and plastic bottles.

When will I see and feel results?

Many users notice their scalp feels cleaner, calmer and less itchy from the very first wash. The fuller, fresher look and reduced shedding tend to build over a few weeks of consistent use. Everyone's different, so we give you a full 60 days to feel the difference — and our guarantee has your back if you don't.

Will it work for my hair type?

Yes — straight, wavy, curly, coily, fine or thick. The bar treats the scalp, not the strand, so it suits every hair type and texture. Whether your scalp runs oily and itchy or dry and flaky, it helps clear buildup and restore balance. It's colour-safe and won't weigh fine hair down.

Does it work for men too?

Definitely. Scalp buildup, itch and thinning-feeling hair aren't a women's-only problem — men deal with all of it. The bar works exactly the same way for everyone, and plenty of guys keep one in the shower for a quick, no-fuss scalp cleanse. One bar, the whole household.

How do I use it?

It takes about a minute. Wet your hair and scalp, then glide the bar directly along your scalp in sections until you feel a light lather. Massage with your fingertips (or the scalp brush included in our bundles) for around 60 seconds to lift buildup and leave your scalp feeling refreshed. Rinse thoroughly, then condition your lengths if you like. Let the bar dry between uses and it'll last for months.
