Most common reasons people write

Before you message — some quick answers.

If your question is here, you can skip the form. Otherwise write to us — these are the four reasons that fill our inbox.

i.

"Where's my order?"

Orders ship in 24h on weekdays. You'll get a tracking link by email. If 5 working days pass without one, write us — we'll resend or refund.

ii.

"I want to refund."

100-day trial, no return required. Email us with your order number; refund processed within 5 working days. Full terms in our refund policy.

iii.

"It's not working."

Warranty covers manufacturing faults for 12 months. Email a short description and a photo or video — we either replace or refund.

iv.

"Can my clinic stock Kalmely?"

We work with a handful of UK headache clinics. Email with subject line starting "Wholesale" — Maya will reply with the trade terms.
