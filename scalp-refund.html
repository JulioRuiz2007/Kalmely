Refund policy.

Last updated · June 30, 2026

Love your scalp or your money back. If you don't love how your scalp feels with the Korean Scalp Revival Bar, we'll refund you in full — and you never have to ship anything back. Just send us a quick email.

The 60-day money-back guarantee

You have 60 days from the date your Kalmely Korean Scalp Revival Bar arrived to decide whether you're happy with how your scalp looks and feels. If at any point in that window you'd like a refund, email us. We'll refund the full price you paid to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.

The refund applies to your order. You keep the bar (or bars) and any free gifts too — there's nothing to box up or send back.

No return required. We don't ask you to repackage the bar, find a box, or stand in line at the post office. Keep it, finish it, or pass it to a friend — your refund is processed either way.

How to request a refund

  1. Email hello@kalmely.com from the address you used to place the order.
  2. Include your order number (it starts with KAL-2026-…) — you'll find it on your confirmation email or thank-you page.
  3. Tell us briefly how the bar worked for your scalp. We use this feedback to improve the product — but you don't need to write more than a sentence.

We'll confirm your refund within one business day, and you'll see the funds returned to your original payment method typically within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Subscribe & Save — how to cancel

Subscribe & Save takes 15% off and auto-refills the same pack you bought when you're due to run low (the 1-bar pack about every 2 months, the 2-bar pack about every 3–4 months, and the 3-bar pack about every 5–6 months). Free gifts are included on your first order only. You can cancel anytime, with no fees and no questions asked:

  • Self-serve customer portal: open the portal link in your order confirmation email to pause, skip, or cancel your subscription in one click.
  • By email: write to hello@kalmely.com and we'll cancel it for you.

If you cancel before your next refill date, you won't be charged again. One-time (non-renewing) packs are also available and never auto-renew.

What's not covered

  • Requests made more than 60 days after delivery.
  • Damage caused by misusing the product against the directions on the label.
  • Bulk or wholesale orders — handled case-by-case with a written agreement.

Refund methods

All refunds are issued in USD ($) to your original payment method via Stripe:

  • Credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex): refund via Stripe — typically 5–10 business days.
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay / Link: refund follows the card linked to your wallet.
  • Amazon Pay: refund follows the payment method on your Amazon account.

Why we run this policy

A scalp routine takes a little patience. Some people notice a cleaner, lighter-feeling scalp within a couple of weeks; others take longer to settle into the habit. We'd rather you keep the bar and give it a fair try than feel pressured to ship it back to claim your money. If it ends up in a drawer, that's still a better outcome than feeling like we held your money hostage.
