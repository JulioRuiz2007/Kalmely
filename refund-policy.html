Refund policy.

Last updated · May 25, 2026

Wart-free or your money back. If you don't love your results, we'll refund you in full — and you never have to ship anything back. Just send us a quick email.

The 6-month money-back guarantee

You have 6 months from the date your Kalmely Tea Tree Oil arrived to decide whether you're happy with your results. If at any point in that window you'd like a refund, email us. We'll refund the full price you paid to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.

The refund applies to your order. You keep the bottle and any free gifts too — there's nothing to box up or send back.

No return required. We don't ask you to repackage the bottle, find a box, or stand in line at the post office. Keep it, finish it, or pass it to a friend — your refund is processed either way.

How to request a refund

  1. Email hello@kalmely.com from the address you used to place the order.
  2. Include your order number (it starts with KAL-2026-…) — you'll find it on your confirmation email or thank-you page.
  3. Tell us briefly how Kalmely worked for you. We use this feedback to improve the product — but you don't need to write more than a sentence.

We'll confirm your refund within one business day, and you'll see the funds returned to your original payment method typically within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

Subscribe & Save — how to cancel

The 100 ml pack (with 4 free gifts) is a Subscribe & Save plan that auto-renews every 2 months at $54.95 with free shipping. You can cancel anytime, with no fees and no questions asked:

  • Self-serve customer portal: open the portal link in your order confirmation email to pause, skip, or cancel your subscription in a few clicks.
  • By email: write to hello@kalmely.com and we'll cancel it for you.

If you cancel before your next renewal date, you won't be charged again. The 30 ml and 60 ml packs are one-time purchases and do not renew.

What's not covered

  • Requests made more than 6 months after delivery.
  • Damage caused by spilling or misusing the product against the directions on the label.
  • Bulk or wholesale orders — handled case-by-case with a written agreement.

Refund methods

All refunds are issued in USD ($) to your original payment method via Stripe:

  • Credit/debit card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex): refund via Stripe — typically 5–10 business days.
  • Apple Pay / Google Pay / Link: refund follows the card linked to your wallet.
  • Amazon Pay: refund follows the payment method on your Amazon account.

Why we run this policy

Clearer skin takes a little patience. Some people see results in a couple of weeks; others take longer. We'd rather you keep the bottle and give it a fair try than feel pressured to ship it back to claim your money. If it ends up in a drawer, that's still a better outcome than feeling like we held your money hostage.
