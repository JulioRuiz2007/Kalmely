Returns & refund policy.

Last updated · June 23, 2026

Feel the difference in 60 days, or your money back. If Kalmely Relief doesn't reach your pain, we'll refund you in full — and you never have to ship anything back. Email or call us.

The 60-day money-back guarantee

You have 60 days from the date your order arrives to decide whether you're happy. If at any point in that window you'd like a refund, just contact us. We'll refund the full price you paid to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days.

Keep the jar. The refund applies to your order and you keep the jar and any free gifts — there's nothing to box up, no return label, no trip to the post office. We'd rather earn your trust than make you mail a jar back.

How to request a refund

  1. Email support@kalmely.com from the address you used to order, or call 1-800-555-0188 (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm ET).
  2. Include your order number — you'll find it on your confirmation email.
  3. Tell us briefly how it worked for you (one sentence is plenty — it helps us improve).

We'll confirm your refund within one business day, and you'll see the funds back on your original payment method typically within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.
